The three bulk meals

Rice and spaghetti are the mains. Potato porridge is the support act and never gets its own week.

Rice

Base

  • Nigerian jollof rice
  • Soy / fried rice
  • Rice + mixed vegetables + diced meat

Flavour

  • Nigerian: tomato, curry, thyme, paprika, seasoning
  • Asian: soy + dark soy + oyster / teriyaki

Spaghetti

Base

  • Jollof spaghetti
  • Teriyaki / honey-garlic spaghetti
  • Soy spaghetti
  • Spaghetti + vegetables + diced meat

Strongest stock position: plenty of spaghetti, macaroni and every sauce already on hand.

Potato porridge

Supporting bulk meal. Paired with whichever main is being eaten, in a smaller batch.

Made with

  • Potato
  • Diced meat
  • Frozen mixed vegetables
  • Celery / bell pepper mix
  • Nigerian seasoning / spices

Vegetables, always on hand

Frozen mixed vegetables (carrot, green beans, corn) and frozen celery + bell pepper mix. Go into all three bulk meals.

Bulk protein

Pre-diced, pre-cooked meat. Mix into the bulk meal, or keep a separate freezer batch (teriyaki / honey-garlic beef) to turn simple food into a proper meal.

Simple foods

Your third option when you want something different. No extra cooking obligation.

Quick savoury

  • Indomie / noodles + 1 to 2 hotdogs
  • Indomie / noodles + egg
  • Hotdog + egg
  • Tuna + crackers / toast
  • Egg + potato

Fruit

  • Apple
  • Orange
  • Banana

Drink

  • Frozen pineapple blended with water
  • Other smoothie additions later, if wanted

Rules
